How it works

Four ways to use Aion

Start with the app — that alone is a complete, private health companion. Everything else is optional, and each piece works on its own.

Start here · No code

The Aion App

Your health dashboard, knowledge graph, and on-device AI — entirely on your phone. Nothing ever leaves your device. Everything below extends this; none of it is required.

✓ HealthKit / Health Connect sync ✓ Vitals, conditions, medications, labs ✓ Interactive knowledge graph ✓ On-device AI (works offline)

App Store & Google Play — coming soon. It runs the local bridge the other pieces connect to.

One command · No install, no server

Your AI & Terminal

A single npx command connects any MCP-compatible AI assistant — and your terminal — straight to your phone’s live FHIR data. No server to run. Ask “What’s my BP trend?” and get answers grounded in your real records.

✓ Claude Code & Desktop, Cursor, Windsurf ✓ Gemini CLI, Zed, Continue.dev & more ✓ Auto-detects & configures every client ✓ Built-in CLI: query your data from a shell
npx @aion-health/bridge setup

Requires Node.js 18+. Phone and PC on the same WiFi (or USB).

Optional · Power users

REST Bridge & Local Models

Want a standing HTTP API and a browser console? Run the open-source aion-open server on your PC and point any tool — curl, notebooks, your own apps — at localhost:3000. Run local models with Ollama. Still fully private.

✓ REST API + browser explorer ✓ Ollama — Llama, Qwen, Gemma, local ✓ Jupyter notebooks & custom dashboards

Most people never need this — the CLI/MCP path covers the common cases without a server.

Mac & Windows · In development

Desktop Companion

A tray app that sends your computer activity to your health graph — anonymized, categories only, on-device. Your phone finally knows your whole story, so the graph can surface correlations like “BP higher on weeks with >6h meetings.”

✓ Screen time, meeting hours, breaks ✓ App categories (never specific names) ✓ Correlates with BP, HRV, sleep, glucose ✗ Never: URLs, app names, content, keystrokes

Available now as source: cd aion-desktop && npm install && npm start · packaged installers coming soon.

Setup

Two steps to connect your AI

The app holds your data; one command does the rest.

Step 1
Your Phone
Required for everything

Open the Aion app (App Store / Google Play — coming soon). It runs the local bridge.

1. Open Settings → Bridge and tap Start 2. Note the 6-digit pairing code (it changes each session)
USB mode (no network needed): switch to the USB tab, then run on your PC — iproxy 8420 8420 iOS  or  adb forward tcp:8420 tcp:8420 Android
Step 2
One Command
AI clients + terminal

The setup wizard finds your phone on the network, prompts for the pairing code, and writes the config for every installed AI client automatically — no server to run.

npx @aion-health/bridge setup

Then restart your AI assistant and ask: “What are my BP trends?” Or query straight from your terminal:

# Counts by resource type npx @aion-health/bridge summary # Query your data (human-readable, or --json) npx @aion-health/bridge query Observation --category vital-signs --limit 20 # A natural-language report over your whole graph npx @aion-health/bridge graph # Export a FHIR bundle · fix a stale config npx @aion-health/bridge export --output health.json npx @aion-health/bridge repair

MCP tools your AI gets: fhir_summary, fhir_query, fhir_get, fhir_record, analyze_health, export_health_data, health_graph_report, health_audit — live data from your phone, not summaries.

Zero Mile Health

Your data. Your control. Your innovation.

Your health data has always been yours. The problem is no one lets you use it. It sits in portals you can barely access, locked in formats you can’t read, controlled by systems that don’t answer to you. Aion changes that. Your data lives on your device, encrypted, private, and queryable — a FHIR knowledge graph that you own completely. On-device AI gives you intelligence that works offline. Go online when you choose, with your own models, on your terms.

But the real power isn’t just access — it’s what you build with it. Correlate your medication adherence with your sleep. Build a dashboard for your family. Visualize your risk trends over time. Ask any AI you trust about your real records. We don’t know what you’ll build for yourself. That’s the point.

We believe essential healthcare should be universal — meeting people where they are. Aion is the start of something different: a digital twin that knows your complete story, and AI that reasons about you — first on your device, then on your network, then with any model you trust. Health at zero miles — in your pocket, understanding you better every day.

This is early. This is open. Build something amazing.
