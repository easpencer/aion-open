One command, no server

Your AI & Terminal

A single npx command connects any MCP-compatible AI assistant — and your terminal — straight to your phone’s live FHIR data. Ask “What’s my BP trend?” and get answers grounded in your real records.

✓ Claude Code & Desktop, Cursor, Windsurf ✓ Gemini CLI, Zed, Continue.dev & more ✓ Auto-detects & configures every client ✓ Built-in CLI: query your data from a shell

Requires Node.js 18+. Phone and PC on the same WiFi (or USB).

Optional · power users

REST Bridge & Local Models

Want a standing HTTP API and a browser console? Run the open-source aion-open server and point any tool — curl, notebooks, your own apps — at localhost:3000. Run local models with Ollama.

✓ REST API + browser explorer ✓ Ollama — Llama, Qwen, Gemma, local ✓ Jupyter notebooks & custom dashboards

Most people never need this — the one-command path covers the common cases.

Mac & Windows · In development

Aion Desktop

A tray app that sends your computer activity to your health graph — anonymized, categories only, on-device. Your graph can then surface correlations like “BP higher on weeks with >6h meetings.”

✓ Screen time, meeting hours, breaks ✓ App categories (never specific names) ✗ Never: URLs, app names, content, keystrokes

Available now as source · packaged installers coming soon.

Zero Mile Health

Your data. Your control. Your innovation.

Your health data has always been yours. The problem is no one lets you use it. It sits in portals you can barely access, locked in formats you can’t read, controlled by systems that don’t answer to you. Aion changes that. Your data lives on your device, encrypted, private, and queryable — a FHIR knowledge graph that you own completely. On-device AI gives you intelligence that works offline. Go online when you choose, with your own models, on your terms.

But the real power isn’t just access — it’s what you build with it. Correlate your medication adherence with your sleep. Build a dashboard for your family. Visualize your risk trends over time. Ask any AI you trust about your real records. We don’t know what you’ll build for yourself. That’s the point.

We believe essential healthcare should be universal — meeting people where they are. Aion is the start of something different: a digital twin that knows your complete story, and AI that reasons about you — first on your device, then on your network, then with any model you trust. Health at zero miles — in your pocket, understanding you better every day.

This is early. This is open. Build something amazing.
