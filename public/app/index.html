What Aion does

Know yourself. Think clearly. Act with confidence.

One private app that holds your whole health story, reasons over it with a panel of frontier AIs, and is there in the moment you actually need help.

Know

Your digital twin

Labs, vitals, meds, conditions, wearables — woven into one private health graph on your phone. It surfaces the connections no single record shows.

Think

The Chorus

Ask a health question and a panel of frontier AIs — Claude, GPT, Gemini — answer together, weighed against graded medical evidence. De-identified before anything leaves your device.

Act

Your Kit

Register the gear you own — a tourniquet, an AED, an EpiPen. When it matters, Aion talks you through the procedure by voice, using validated clinical protocols. Fully offline.

Aion guides; it does not replace emergency services. In a life-threatening emergency, call your local emergency number.
